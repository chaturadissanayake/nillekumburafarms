Our Story

A family farm.
Nothing more, nothing less.

We started with a small flock of chickens in our backyard and a belief that Kandy families deserve food they can actually trust.

Every egg is collected fresh each morning. Every bird is reared in a clean, carefully monitored shelter. We manage our flock closely to ensure safety and quality. This is not a factory. It's our home.

We believe that caring for the land and caring for the community are not separate things. That's why we farm the way we do, not because it's easy, but because it's right.

How We Farm

Grown with intention.

We don't use the word sustainable lightly. Here's what it means in practice, every day, on this farm.

Safe & Structured.

Our birds are housed in secure, structured environments designed to protect them from the elements and predators, ensuring a consistent and safe supply.

Clean inputs only.

We maintain strict hygiene and feed standards. Animal waste is composted and returned to the land, keeping the local soil cycle intact.

Short supply chain.

By selling directly, no distributor, no cold storage, no supermarket markup, we cut food miles, reduce packaging waste, and keep income within the Menikhinna community.
